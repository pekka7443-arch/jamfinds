Version 2.1 Changelog
Texture Fixes:
Fixed texture issues with the rear-wheel-drive suspension.
Seat Fixes:
Fixed issues with the vehicle's seats.
Optimization:
The mod has been further optimized for better performance while maintaining its visual quality. A highly detailed and optimized Ibishu Covet pack with an extensive amount of customization, interior and exterior options, and various configurations designed to give the vehicle a wide range of different styles.

Features
  • Interior Customization: Extensive interior customization with 7 custom floor mats and 13 different steering wheels.
  • Interior Lighting: Custom ambient lighting for the interior, trunk, and leg area. Available in red, purple, yellow, blue, green, white, and cyan.
  • Little Trees Air Fresheners: 5 Little Trees air freshener pendants with different designs.
  • Stickers: 2 different custom sticker options.
  • Trunk Customization: Additional trunk items, including a subwoofer and wheels.
  • VIP Table: Custom VIP table setup with a variety of food, drinks, and other items.
  • Taillights: Clear and Euro-style taillight options.
  • Headlights: Custom Battle Eye headlight option.
  • Body Kit & Exhaust: Custom BN Type body kit with 2 different custom exhausts.
  • Glass Tint: 3 different window tint levels: 100%, 50%, and 5%.
  • Intakes & Intercooler: Custom intake options with a custom intercooler.
  • Manual Shifters: 5 custom manual shifters, including Mugen, R-Magic, Thrash Racing, and Bubble designs.
  • Wheels: 5 custom 4-lug wheel designs, available in 15x8 and 16x7 sizes: SSR Longchamp, SSR MK3, Work Equip 03, Work Equip 40, Work Meister CR01.
  • Mirrors: 2 custom mirror options inspired by Ganador and Craft Square designs.
  • Door Panel Inserts: Custom door panel inserts with 5 different skins, including 4 flame designs and 1 checkered design.
  • Dashboard: Custom Defi sensors installed on the dashboard for additional interior detail.
  • RWD & Drift: Added RWD drivetrain and a dedicated Drift configuration.
